NIRIKSH System Guidelines & Workflow Architecture

Governance Note: Automated rule evaluations and AI-assisted suggestions support officer scrutiny; all approval, clarification, and rejection decisions are recorded by authorised AICTE officers.

1. Two-Portal Separation: The Institution / College Portal allows applicant colleges to submit applications, upload PDF dossiers, track the 5-stage progress bar, and respond to clarification requests. The AICTE / Government Portal enables scrutiny officers and supervisors to verify documents, run compliance checks, manage EVC inspections, and record decisions.

2. Parallel Compliance Architecture: Document Processing (PyMuPDF/OCR) extracts page-indexed fields → Parallel AI-Assisted Analysis (RAG) + Deterministic Rule Engine evaluate compliance → Findings Summary presents combined evidence → Authorised Officer records the decision.

3. Governance Guardrail: AI suggestions are never treated as verified regulatory facts, and AI never approves or rejects an application.
